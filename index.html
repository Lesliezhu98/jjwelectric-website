About Us

Reliable Electrical Accessories Manufacturer

Shanghai JJW Electric and Instrument Engineering Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer and supplier of electrical and instrumentation products, serving customers worldwide.

Who We Are

JJW Electric and Instrument Engineering Limited is a manufacturer and developer offering high-performance clamping, fastening, and bundling products to diverse markets. Our main products include stainless steel cable ties, stainless steel cable cleats, nylon cable ties, cable terminals, and more.

With our rolling factory located in Ningbo, Zhejiang Province, we provide high-quality products and customized solutions to customers in oil & gas, petrochemical, pharmaceutical, marine, and railway industries worldwide.
