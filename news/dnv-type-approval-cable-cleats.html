JJW Stainless Steel Cable Cleats Awarded DNV Type Approval

Recently, JJW Electric and Instrument Engineering Limited announced that its self-developed and manufactured Stainless Steel Cable Cleat series has successfully obtained DNV (Det Norske Veritas) Type Approval.

About DNV Certification

DNV (Det Norske Veritas) is a world-leading professional risk management and quality assurance organization with high authority in offshore engineering, shipbuilding, oil & gas and other industries. Obtaining DNV Type Approval means that the product design, materials, manufacturing process and performance all meet stringent international standard requirements.

Certified Product Range

The products awarded DNV certification include:
• STCC-V Trefoil Cable Cleats
• STCC-D Double Cable Cleats
• STCC-S Single Cable Cleats

All products are manufactured from 304/316 stainless steel, comply with IEC 61914 standard requirements, and have passed short-circuit current testing, mechanical strength testing and corrosion resistance testing.

Significance of Certification

1. International Recognition: DNV certification is an authoritative certification widely recognized in the global offshore engineering and marine industries, providing strong support for products entering international markets.
2. Quality Assurance: The certification process covers full-process quality control from raw material procurement to finished product inspection, ensuring product quality stability and reliability.
3. Customer Confidence: DNV certification enhances customer trust in our products, especially in high-end applications such as offshore platforms, ships, and offshore wind power.

Application Areas

DNV-certified cable cleats can be widely applied in:
• Offshore oil platforms
• Ships and offshore engineering
• Offshore wind power
• Ports and terminal facilities
• Petrochemical and natural gas projects
