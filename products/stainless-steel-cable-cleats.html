Selection Steps

1
Identify Cable Type

Determine whether single-core or multi-core cables are used, and the cable outer diameter.

2
Determine Cable Arrangement

For single-core cables, confirm flat formation or trefoil arrangement.

3
Know Short Circuit Current

Understand the available short circuit current (RMS or peak) to select appropriate cleat strength.

4
Installation Notes

Bend radius should be 8-12 times cable diameter. Cleat spacing at bends should not exceed 0.3m. Refer to installation guide for detailed specs.
