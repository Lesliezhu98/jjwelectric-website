Lighting Series

The lighting series includes LED street light luminaires, solar photovoltaic panels and integrated solar street lights. LED street lights use high-quality chips with high luminous efficiency and long lifespan. Solar street lights integrate solar panels, lithium batteries and LED luminaires for energy-efficient outdoor lighting of roads, parks and industrial areas.

Selection Steps

1
Identify Application

Select appropriate power and height based on lighting application (road, industrial park, etc.).

2
Choose Lighting Solution

Choose LED street light if grid power available, or solar street light for off-grid or energy-saving needs.

3
Determine Backup Days

Select battery capacity based on local rainy weather conditions.

4
Installation Notes

Ensure solid pole foundation. Mount solar panels facing south for maximum sunlight exposure.
