Selection Steps

1
Determine Tube Size

Measure tube or cable outer diameter to select matching clip size.

2
Select Liner Material

NBR rubber for hydraulic/oil lines. PVC for general cable applications.

3
Select Band Material

Galvanized steel for indoor dry. Stainless steel for outdoor/corrosive.

4
Installation Notes

Ensure full contact between clip and tube. Follow bolt torque requirements.
