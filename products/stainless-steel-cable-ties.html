Detailed Selection Guide

Stainless steel cable ties are made from high-quality 304 or 316 stainless steel, offering excellent corrosion resistance and high temperature performance. Ideal for outdoor and harsh environment applications in petrochemical, marine and power industries. Available in ball lock, ladder lock, wing lock types with epoxy, PVC and other coatings.

Ball Lock Type

Ball lock is the most common type of stainless steel cable tie with self-locking design for fast and easy installation. Suitable for general industrial cable bundling.

Selection Steps

1
Select Material

Choose 304 or 316 stainless steel based on environment corrosivity. Use 316 for salt spray environments.

2
Select Lock Type

Ball lock general purpose, ladder lock low profile, wing lock adjustable.

3
Determine Size

Select width and length based on cable bundle diameter, ensuring sufficient extra length.

4
Coating Options

Choose epoxy or PVC coated for outdoor or corrosive environments.
