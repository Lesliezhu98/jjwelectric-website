GRP Sunshades

GRP (Glass Reinforced Plastic) sunshades are specifically designed for field instruments, protecting them from direct sunlight, rain and extreme temperatures. Made of 3mm thick GRP, they are lightweight, corrosion-resistant and UV-stable. Multiple mounting types and sizes available, suitable for oil & gas, petrochemical and chemical industrial sites.

DD1-DD2 Instrument Sunshades
DD3-DD4 Instrument Sunshades
DD5-DD6 Detector Sunshades
DD7-DD8 Instrument Sunshades
DD9 Instrument Sunshade

Detailed Selection Guide

GRP instrument sunshades are manufactured from 3mm thick glass reinforced plastic, offering excellent corrosion resistance, UV stability and high strength-to-weight ratio. Designed to protect field-mounted pressure transmitters, temperature instruments, flow meters and other devices from direct sunlight (preventing measurement errors and aging) and rain. Supplied with SS316 stainless steel U-bolts and mounting brackets for easy installation.

Selection Steps

1
Identify Instrument Type

Choose based on instrument: standard transmitters use DD1-DD4/DD7-DD9, flame/gas detectors use DD5/DD6.

2
Determine Mounting Type

Select back mount, side mount, bottom mount or top mount based on site conditions.

3
Confirm Dimensions

Select appropriate size based on instrument outline dimensions for full coverage.

4
Pipe Size

Standard mounting on 2 inch NPS pipe with U-bolt fixing.

5
Material Confirmation
