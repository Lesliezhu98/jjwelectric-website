Selection Steps

1
Select Mounting Type

Choose self-standing, wall mount or pipe mount based on site conditions.

2
Determine Pipe Size & Height

Select appropriate pipe size and stand height based on instrument dimensions and mounting height requirements.

3
Choose Material

Standard is carbon steel hot-dip galvanized. 304/316 stainless steel or aluminum optional.

4
Installation Notes

Ensure solid foundation and proper bolt torque. Periodically inspect galvanized coating integrity for outdoor use.
