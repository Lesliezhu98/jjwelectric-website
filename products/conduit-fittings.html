Selection Steps

1
Determine Conduit Size

Select conduit size (1/2 inch to 4 inch) based on conductor cross-section and quantity.

2
Determine Turn Direction

Choose Type C (through), LB (down), LL (left), LR (right) based on routing direction.

3
Verify Environment

Galvanized for indoor/outdoor. Use sealing gaskets for wet locations.

4
Matching Fittings

Order compatible couplings, locknuts and bushings together.
