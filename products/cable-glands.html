Cable Glands

Cable glands are manufactured from polyamide 6 with glass fiber reinforcement, halogen-free and UV resistant. Available in metric (M12-M63) and PG threads (9PG-21PG), IP68 protection rating. Suitable for cable entry sealing in various industrial electrical equipment.

Detailed Selection Guide

Cable glands are devices used to seal and secure cables passing through equipment enclosures, while providing dust and water protection. This series is made from polyamide 6 with glass fiber reinforcement, halogen-free and UV resistant. Compliant with DIN VDE 0619, IP68 protection rating (5 bar). Available in metric and PG thread types, multiple color options.

Selection Steps

1
Identify Cable OD

Measure cable outer diameter range to select matching gland clamping range.

2
Determine Thread Type

Choose metric (M series) or PG thread based on equipment hole size. Confirm thread compatibility.

3
Select Color

Standard black, available in gray, white and red. Choose based on equipment appearance and environment.

4
Protection Requirements

Confirm environmental protection rating requirements. IP68 for outdoor and humid environments.

5
Installation Notes

Ensure proper seal contact with cable and enclosure. Follow torque requirements for sealing performance.
